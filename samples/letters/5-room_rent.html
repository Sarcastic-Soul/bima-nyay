Reason for deduction: The room rent limit under your policy is 1% of the sum insured per day, i.e. Rs 5,000/- per day. You occupied a Deluxe room charged at Rs 9,000/- per day. As the room chosen is costlier than the entitled room, the insurer has applied a proportionate deduction of 4/9 (44.44%) on room-rent-linked charges such as surgeon, anaesthetist, OT and nursing charges. The amount of Rs 1,64,600/- is admitted against the claimed amount of Rs 2,48,600/-.

Clause 3.7 (Room Rent and Proportionate Deduction): "If the insured is admitted to a room whose rent exceeds the eligible limit, the Company's liability for room rent and all expenses that are linked to the room category shall be reduced in the proportion of the eligible room rent to the actual room rent."

The balance of Rs 84,000/- is payable by you directly to the hospital. Payment of Rs 1,64,600/- has been released to the hospital.
