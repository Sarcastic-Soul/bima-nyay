Why we cannot pay

Cataract surgery is covered under your policy only after a specific waiting period of 24 months of continuous cover. Your cover began on 05/03/2024, so on the date of admission (22/07/2025) you had completed 16 months and 17 days. The waiting period for cataract ends on 04/03/2026. The claim is therefore not payable at this time.

Clause 4.3 (Specific Waiting Periods) of your policy states:

"The following conditions are not covered during the first 24 months of continuous coverage: cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hysterectomy for menorrhagia, joint replacement unless due to accident, and calculus diseases of the urinary system."

This waiting period applies to all treatment of cataract, irrespective of whether the condition was known before the policy started. Treatment of this or the other eye taken after 04/03/2026 will be considered as per the policy terms. Your cover for all other illnesses continues as usual.
