> During the year, all 17 Insurance Ombudsman centres together received a total 52575 complaints and disposed of 49705 complaints out of which 12855 were treated as non-entertainable.

> Majority of complaints in the Health Insurance sector were reported under Rule 13(1) (b) – any partial or total repudiation of claims by an Insurer. This accounts for 95% of the total entertainable complaints disposed during the year.

Rule 13(1)(b) is "any partial or total repudiation of claims by an insurer".

Statement H5 (health insurance, recommendations and awards in favour of complainants), "Total" row: 10,135 recommendations worth 8,917.53 lakh; 6,233 awards worth 5,875.26 lakh; combined 16,368 cases and 14,792.79 lakh (about Rs 148 crore; 1 crore = 100 lakh).

## What these numbers do not show

They count complaints that reached an Ombudsman. They do not count families who were rejected and never complained, so they understate the problem.
